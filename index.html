Where does our family data go?

Family records stay on your device. There are no TubTime accounts, ads or cloud sync. Android backup may copy app data according to your device settings. Core logging works offline.

How do reminders work?

Free offers a daily reminder around 6 pm or a wash-gap reminder around 48 hours after a logged wash. Plus adds custom hours and messages. Timing is approximate and depends on Android notification permissions and battery settings.

Are there parent controls?

Settings, purchases and data controls sit behind a simple parent question. It is a gate, not authentication. You can delete wash logs and reset local history in the app.

Which phones can use TubTime?

Android 8.0 and later. TubTime logs after washing; it doesn’t time a bath or shower. There is no released iPhone app.
