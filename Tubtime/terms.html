TubTime Terms of Service

Simple terms for a simple, offline-first family bath-time app.

Effective date

14 October 2026

Using TubTime

TubTime is provided as a family-friendly tool for tracking baths and showers, setting local reminders, and making routines more playful. A parent or guardian should manage settings, purchases, and device permissions.

Offline app behaviour

TubTime does not require accounts, login, cloud sync, or a backend service. App data is stored locally on the device unless the device owner uses platform backup tools.

Purchases

TubTime Plus is a one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchase, not a subscription. Google Play displays the current local price before payment. Buying or restoring Plus requires Google Play and internet access. TubTime Plus is handled through Google Play Billing. Purchase availability, refunds, and payment handling follow Google Play policies.

Reminders

Reminder notifications are local device notifications. TubTime cannot guarantee delivery if device battery, notification, focus, or operating-system settings block them.

No medical advice

TubTime is a routine and motivation app. It does not provide medical, hygiene, or parenting advice. Families should use their own judgement for bath and shower routines.
