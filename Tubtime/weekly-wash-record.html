Weekly bath & shower record

Write down real washes after they happen. Use only the rows you need. This sheet is a record, not a schedule or advice about washing frequency.

To print: use your browser’s Print menu (Ctrl+P on Windows, Command+P on Mac). Choose A4 or Letter, portrait, and turn off browser headers and footers. You can also choose Save as PDF. Handwritten entries stay on your paper copy. These are blank paper spaces, not online fields.
