Email support ↗
Is TubTime a bath timer?

TubTime logs baths and showers after washing. It doesn’t measure how long a wash takes. Only log washes that have really happened.

Can I use TubTime without paying?

Yes. The free app includes one profile, bath and shower logging, level, XP, streak, ten recent logs, standard reminders and the Sunny Duck theme. Plus is an optional one-time purchase with no subscription.

How can I try the Plus features?

Open the sample-family demo in the app. It has separate local records and settings, no time limit and no purchase requirement. Demo reminders aren’t sent. Exit the demo to return to your own family; exploring the demo doesn’t buy Plus.

Does it need an account or internet?

No TubTime account is needed, and core logging works offline. Google Play purchases and restoring Plus need internet access. There is no TubTime cloud sync, including with Plus. Android backups can apply according to your device settings.

When will reminders arrive?

Free offers a daily reminder around 6 pm or a wash-gap reminder around 48 hours after a logged wash. Plus adds custom hours and messages. All reminders are approximate: Android notification permissions and battery settings affect delivery.

Is TubTime available for iPhone?

TubTime is currently available for Android 8.0 and later on Google Play. There is no released iPhone app to download.

Does TubTime tell us how often to wash?

No. Families choose their own routines. TubTime is a routine logger, not a medical device, and doesn’t give medical advice. Ask a qualified professional if you have medical questions.
